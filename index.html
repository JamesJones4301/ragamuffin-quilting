Who's behind the needle

Meet Patsey — a disabled Vietnam veteran with a needle and a lot of faith.

Patsey is a disabled Vietnam veteran in her eighties who serves the Lord faithfully with whatever loaves and fishes she has on hand — in her case, a sewing machine and a lifetime of scrap fabric. A while back she turned that faithfulness into a small business, cutting and piecing leftover fabric — old shirts, extra yardage, donated remnants — into quilts and pillows that had nowhere else to go and everything left to give.

"GOD takes a Ragamuffin person and forgives them, and turns them into someone beautiful. Ragamuffin Quilting is taking scraps of fabric and turning them into a useful item of beauty — crafted by a veteran woman."

Every quilt Patsey makes is worth so much more than what she charges for it. If you've ever heard Dolly Parton's song Coat of Many Colors, you already know exactly what we mean — give her a call if you'd like her to make one for you.
